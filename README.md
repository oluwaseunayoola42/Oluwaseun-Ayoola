## 📌 Projects

### 1. [Student Performance Analysis](https://github.com/oluwaseunayoola42/Student-Performance-Analysis)

Tested whether platform activity predicts learning for 36 adult ESOL learners at Klik2learn. 81% improved from Mid-Term to Final, and the activity flags worked as usage monitoring but not as an academic-risk signal. Recommendations delivered for the provider's dashboard.
**Tools:** Python, SQL, Looker Studio

### 2. [Embarkation Point Daily Activities](https://github.com/oluwaseunayoola42/Embarkation-Point-Daily-Activities)

Power BI report on 193,871 personnel movements across offshore oil and gas embarkation points in Nigeria (Jul to Nov 2021). One hub handled about 59% of all traffic, September was the peak month, and over 3,300 personnel moved through with compliance issues.
**Tools:** Power BI

### 3. [AI Impact on Employment Dashboard](https://github.com/oluwaseunayoola42/AI-Impact-on-Employment-Dashboard)

Excel dashboard on how AI may affect jobs from 2026 to 2030: automation risk by role, industry disruption, hiring by country, and education vs salary. Currently being redesigned.
**Tools:** Excel
